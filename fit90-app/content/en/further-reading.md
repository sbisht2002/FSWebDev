# Further reading

Trusted books and websites if you'd like to go deeper. We link to them; we never host copies.

## Websites

- [NHS: Erection problems (erectile dysfunction)](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/erection-problems-erectile-dysfunction/): clear overview of causes and treatment.
- [NHS: Ejaculation problems](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/ejaculation-problems/): early, delayed and other ejaculation concerns.
- [Mayo Clinic: Kegel exercises for men](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/mens-health/in-depth/kegel-exercises-for-men/art-20045074): how to do pelvic-floor exercises.
- [European Association of Urology: Sexual and Reproductive Health guidelines](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/sexual-and-reproductive-health): the professional guidelines doctors use (technical).
- [ISSM: Sexual Health Q&A](https://www.issm.info): patient information from the International Society for Sexual Medicine.
- [Tele-MANAS](https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in): India's free national mental-health helpline, 14416.
- [WHO: Physical activity guidelines](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128): how much activity adults need.

## Books

- **The New Male Sexuality** by Bernie Zilbergeld (Bantam, revised edition 1999). A classic, myth-busting guide to men's sexuality.
- **Coping with Premature Ejaculation** by Michael E. Metz and Barry W. McCarthy (New Harbinger, 2003). A practical program for men and couples.
- **Coping with Erectile Dysfunction** by Barry W. McCarthy and Michael E. Metz (New Harbinger, 2004). Practical help for men and their partners.
- **Mind Over Mood** (2nd edition) by Dennis Greenberger and Christine A. Padesky (Guilford Press, 2015). A widely used CBT workbook for anxiety and low mood.
- **Self-Compassion** by Kristin Neff (William Morrow, 2011). An accessible introduction to treating yourself with kindness.
- **Full Catastrophe Living** by Jon Kabat-Zinn (revised edition, Bantam, 2013). The foundations of mindfulness-based stress reduction.

Look for these through your local bookshop or library.
