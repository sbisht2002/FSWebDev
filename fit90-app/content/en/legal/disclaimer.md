# Medical disclaimer

Fit90 provides general education about men's sexual health, fitness, diet and wellbeing. It is **not medical advice** and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified health professional.

- Nothing in the app, its articles, audio sessions, PDF guides or optional AI coach is a diagnosis, a prescription, or a recommendation of any medicine or supplement.
- Always talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program if you have a heart condition, chest pain, high blood pressure or other health problems.
- Stop any exercise that causes pain, and seek medical advice.
- Never stop or change a prescribed medicine without talking to your doctor.
- If you have a medical emergency, including chest pain or an erection lasting more than 4 hours, call **112** immediately.
- If you are in emotional distress or thinking about harming yourself, call **Tele-MANAS on 14416** (free, any time) or **112** in an emergency.

The evidence behind sexual-health techniques varies. Pelvic-floor training for early ejaculation and erection problems is promising but supported mainly by small, low-to-moderate-quality studies. Behavioural techniques help some men and work best alongside medical care. Evidence for mindfulness approaches in men is limited. Performance anxiety has no single proven treatment. Articles list their sources and when they were last reviewed.

Fitness targets (calories, protein, water and training volume) are estimates from standard formulas. They are not suitable for people with eating disorders, kidney disease, diabetes treated with insulin, or other conditions that need individual medical or dietetic advice.

By using the app you accept that you are responsible for how you use this information, and that you will seek professional help when you need it.
