# Privacy policy

*Plain-language version. Last updated 24 October 2026.*

We built Fit90 so that your most private information stays private. This policy explains what happens to your data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## The short version

- **No account.** You never give us your name, email, phone number or any login.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Your answers, plan, progress, journal, logs, check-ins and settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database.
- **No ads, no trackers, no analytics.** The app contains no advertising or analytics software and does not track you.
- **You're in control.** You can export all your data or delete everything at any time from Settings.

## What is stored on your device

- Your onboarding answers, including goals and health check answers
- Your optional body profile (age, height, weight, target weight, goal)
- Your 8-week plan edits, completed sessions, ratings, notes, XP and streaks
- Your thought journal, practice logs and weekly check-ins
- Your bookmarks and app settings (theme, reminders, sound)
- Your fitness program progress and water log

Sensitive data is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held in your phone's secure keystore. Cloud backup of the app's data is turned off, so it is not copied to your Google account.

## What leaves your device

**By default, nothing.** The standard version of the app does not even request permission to use the internet.

**Optional AI coach.** If a version of the app with the AI coach is installed and you choose to use it, the messages you type and a short summary of your plan (such as your current level and streak, never your name or contact details) are sent through our secure server to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model, to generate a reply. We use an anonymous random ID only to apply a daily message limit. Our server does not store the content of your messages; it keeps only aggregate counts (for example, how many messages were sent in a day). Your chat history is stored only on your phone and you can clear it any time. Anthropic processes messages under its own privacy policy.

**Your own actions.** If you choose to export your data, save or share a PDF, or open an external link, the file or link goes wherever you choose to send it.

## Notifications

Reminders use neutral wording such as "Time for your 10-minute session". They are created on your phone; no reminder data is sent anywhere.

## Your rights under the DPDP Act

You have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

- **Access:** use *Settings > Export my data* to get a complete copy in a readable file.
- **Correct:** retake the quiz or edit your body profile in Settings.
- **Erase:** use *Settings > Delete everything*. This permanently deletes your data and the encryption key from your phone.
- **Withdraw consent:** stop using any feature, turn off the coach, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also removes all data.

Because we do not hold your data on our servers, we cannot recover it for you if you delete it or lose your phone.

## Children

Fit90 is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update it in the app and change the date above. For questions or grievances, contact the developer through the app's store listing.
