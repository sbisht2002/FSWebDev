Most of the time, worries about erections, lasting longer or desire are common, understandable and very workable. This app can help with a lot of that. But some signs mean your body is asking for a proper check-up. Seeing a doctor is not a failure. It is often the fastest way to feel better, and sometimes it protects your heart and long-term health too.

## Go to emergency care now (call 112)

- **An erection that lasts more than 4 hours**, whether or not it is painful. This is called priapism. It can permanently damage the tissue that makes erections possible, so it needs urgent treatment. Don't wait for it to settle on its own.
- **Chest pain, pressure or tightness**, especially with sweating, sickness or breathlessness. Stop any activity and call for help.
- **Sudden severe pain or swelling in the testicles.** This can be a twisted testicle, which needs surgery within hours.

## See a doctor soon (within days to weeks)

**Erection problems that are new or came on suddenly.** A change from your usual pattern deserves a look. Causes can include blood-vessel problems, hormones, medicines, stress, or a mix.

**Erection problems if you have diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, heart disease, or you smoke.** The blood vessels in the penis are small, so they often show trouble before the larger vessels around the heart do. Large reviews have linked erectile dysfunction with a higher risk of future heart disease. A check of your blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol can protect far more than your sex life.

**Chest pain or breathlessness when you exert yourself.** Get checked before starting a new exercise plan.

**Pain, blood in your urine or semen, a lump, or a new bend that is painful or makes sex difficult.** These need a physical examination. A painful bend can be a condition called Peyronie's disease, which is easier to manage early.

**Lifelong early ejaculation that bothers you or your partner a lot.** In European urology guidelines, medicines are the first-line treatment for lifelong early ejaculation. The techniques in this app can work well alongside them, so it isn't one or the other.

**Low desire together with tiredness, low mood or poor sleep.** This can come from low testosterone, thyroid problems, sleep apnoea, depression or medicines. A doctor can check with simple tests.

**Side effects from medicines.** Some medicines for blood pressure, depression, anxiety, prostate or hair loss can affect erections, ejaculation or desire. Please don't stop any medicine on your own. Ask your doctor whether a change is possible.

**Pelvic pain or urinary problems.** Stop pelvic-floor exercises if they hurt, and see a doctor or a pelvic-floor physiotherapist. In this app your trainer will switch to a gentle, relaxation-first version.

## When your mind needs support

Anxiety, low mood, relationship stress, or feeling hopeless are good reasons to talk to someone. Sexual worries and mental health often feed each other. A psychologist, psychiatrist, or a qualified sex therapist can help, and these conversations are confidential.

If you ever feel you might harm yourself, please reach out right away. In India, **Tele-MANAS (14416)** gives free, confidential mental-health support at any hour. In an emergency, call **112**.

## Who to see in India

- **General physician or family doctor:** a good first stop for most concerns, including blood pressure, sugar and medicines.
- **Urologist or andrologist:** specialists in erections, ejaculation, testicles, the prostate and pain.
- **Psychiatrist or psychologist:** for anxiety, depression and stress.
- **Qualified sex therapist:** for performance anxiety, couple concerns and behavioural techniques. Look for proper training and registration.
- **Pelvic-floor physiotherapist:** for pelvic pain or help learning pelvic-floor exercises correctly.

## Getting the most from an appointment

It can feel awkward to bring this up. Doctors talk about erections and ejaculation every day, and they won't be shocked. It helps to write down a few notes first:

- When the problem started and whether it is always there or only sometimes
- Whether you get erections on waking or when alone
- Any medicines, supplements, alcohol, smoking or recreational drugs
- Other health conditions, and how your sleep and mood have been

You can also show your doctor your progress page from this app, if you like.
